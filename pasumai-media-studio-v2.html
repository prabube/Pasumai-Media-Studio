📘 Learning Guide

Make your first song video in about 10 minutes. Follow the steps — tap “Take me there” to jump to the right place.

1

Add your song

Open the Media tab and choose an mp3 under “Song”. This sets the length of your video.

2

Choose a background

Pick one or more video clips (they loop until the song ends) or switch to Images for a slideshow with zoom and pan. Use ↑ ↓ to change the order, and the sliders or number boxes to resize, move or fade each one.

3

Add subtitles

In the Text tab: paste lyrics in SRT or LRC format, load a subtitle file, or press Generate to create them from the audio (Tamil, Tanglish or English). Then fix any mistakes in the box.

4

Style your text

In Style, pick a font (there are handwriting fonts for English and Tamil), colour, outline, position and an animation such as Typewriter or Karaoke. You can also drag the text on the video.

5

Add logos and extra text

Add logo or sticker images under Media → Overlay images. Add titles and credits as Text boxes in the Text tab. Each has size, position, rotation, opacity and timing.

6

Preview

Press Play (or the space bar). Drag the bar to jump around. Use the volume sliders to balance the song and clip sound.

7

Export

In Export, choose Full HD, set start and end if you want only part, and press Download video. It records in real time, so keep the tab open. Use Save current frame for a high-quality picture.

8

Thumbnail and YouTube

Make a cover in Cover, then use YouTube to write a title, description, tags and keywords. Open YouTube Studio and paste them with your video.

9

Save your work

Give your project a name above the video, then use Export → Save project. Next time choose Open Project on the home screen.

💡 Quick tips

  1. Space = play / pause. ← and → = jump 5 seconds.
  2. Every slider has a number box — type an exact value.
  3. On a phone, use the tabs at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Light or dark theme: tap the 🌓 button.
  5. Admins can add users and shared links from the ⚙️ button.
  6. Files already in your cloud are never uploaded or downloaded twice.
